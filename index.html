MCU Fandom Fan Page

Website Description

The MCU Fandom Page is dedicated to exploring the Marvel Cinematic Universe, with the focus on watching the individual films/tv shows that connect in a shared storyline. These shared storylines come together in the Avengers films. I enjoy these films mostly because of the character, Tony Stark, played by Robert Downey Jr., who will be returning as the main villain, Dr. Doom. In order to prepare for Avengers: Doomsday, I want to look at the previous MCU installments, and rank their importance for the upcoming Avengers film as I watch them to get call caught up.

  • Home page introducing the Marvel Cinematic Universe and Avengers films
  • Top 5 page ranking the most important MCU movies in preparation for Avengers: Doomsday
  • MCU trivia, facts, and an Avengers:Doomsday trailer
