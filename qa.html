MCU Q&A

Question 1:

Will Doctor Doom replace Kang as the main villain?

Absolutely. This film was orginally called AvengersL Kang Wars, but Marvel has shifted focus toward Doctor Doom as the primary multiverse threat. The catalyst being a public legal issue from the the actor, Jonathan Majors, who played Kang the Conqueror. But also, the lack of interest and poor reception everything post Avengers: Endgame. By introducing Doom, and having him be played by Robert Downey Jr., they can bring in a fan favorite character and also use nostalgia, to fill seats for Avengers: Doomsday.

Question 2:

Why does Doctor Doom like like Tony Stark?

This is one of the biggest mysteries surrounding the film, and it's been guarded very closely. We only have teaster trailers so far, but will stay up to date on the release of trailers, merchandise, interviews, etc., to answer this question.
